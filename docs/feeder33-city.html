The city

A working evening on a real distribution feeder. You start at the desk in the control room with four charging stations and 287 vehicles, and every lamp, meter and instrument in the city is lit by the same power-flow solution.

WASD walks, Shift runs, E uses what you are facing, F acknowledges alarms. Click the view to look with the mouse — or just give it focus and use the arrow keys to turn, which needs no mouse at all. Every bus is also listed as live text below the controls.

    Whether to believe any of this

    The city runs the same backward/forward sweep as the offline engine. On load it re-solves every recorded control step and compares the two, so the numbers on the meters are checked rather than trusted. Two of the four candidates are labelled stand-ins whose recorded episodes are still to be exported from the thesis notebook; they are marked as such everywhere they appear.
