What the GPU is actually doing here

Everything in this list is impossible in the canvas build, and none of it changes a single number.

Lighting

Real inverse-square falloff

Each lamp is a PointLight in candela, driven by the same V3.4 law. The light wraps over kerbs, car roofs and pavement because it is being integrated per pixel — in the canvas version it was an ellipse painted on the ground.

Post-processing

Bloom that carries the voltage

A healthy lamp blooms wide; a sagging one barely glows. The halo size becomes a second channel for the same number. Drag the slider to see how much of the read comes from it.

Shadows

Only two lights cast them

A shadow-casting point light is six render passes. Thirty-three of them is not something any browser will do, so the two nearest lamps cast and the rest do not — a swap you cannot see, and the reason this holds frame rate.

Geometry

Instancing, and hanging wire

Every building is one draw call, every window another. The conductors are tubes along a curve with real sag, instead of straight lines — which is what a span between two poles actually does.
