\section{Selected Projects}

\entry{Droop Control in Decentralized Inverter-Based AC, Islanded, and DC Microgrids}{July 2023}
Power Systems Laboratory (EEE 306), Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology \quad|\quad Team of 5
\begin{itemize}
  \item Modeled decentralized droop control across three microgrid architectures --- grid-connected AC, islanded AC, and DC with hybrid battery/supercapacitor storage --- in MATLAB/Simulink, driving three-phase two-level VSCs through cascaded voltage and current loops at 10~kHz SPWM
  \item Achieved equal active and reactive power sharing across three parallel inverters rated 500~kW, 300~kW, and 200~kW from local measurements alone, with no inter-unit communication link
  \item Held bus frequency within 59.7--60.3~Hz under 1\% P/f droop and regulated voltage under 4\% Q/V droop on a 600~V\textsubscript{rms}, 60~Hz islanded network
\end{itemize}
\repo{https://github.com/Rifat137710/Droop-Control-in-Decentralized-Inverter-based-AC-Islanded-DC-Microgrid-}

\entry{Intelligent Mobile Manipulation System for Daily Assistance of Visually Impaired Individuals}{July 2025}
Robotics and Automation Laboratory (EEE 404), Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology \quad|\quad Team of 6
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built an autonomous assistive robot on a Raspberry Pi 4B executing a full retrieve-and-deliver loop: workspace mapping, target localization, navigation, grasping with a 6-DOF arm, and delivery
  \item Designed a two-layer perception stack pairing an overhead camera with ArUco fiducials for global localization against a front-facing Pi Camera running on-device classification for close-range object verification
  \item Closed the approach loop with HC-SR04 ultrasonic feedback to a 32~cm standoff, characterizing $\pm$1--2~cm ranging error across surface textures
\end{itemize}
\repo{https://github.com/Rifat137710/Development-of-an-Intelligent-Mobile-Manipulation-System-}

\entry{Versatile CNC Machine: Pen Plotter, Laser Cutter, and Engraver}{July 2024}
Control Systems Laboratory, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology \quad|\quad Team of 5
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a CoreXY three-in-one CNC platform supporting interchangeable pen, laser, and cutting toolheads, driven by an Arduino UNO running GRBL with A4988 micro-stepping drivers on dual stepper motors
  \item Ported two GRBL builds to cover the toolchange --- v0.9 with servo support for Z-axis pen lift, v1.1h in laser mode for engraving --- and integrated an Inkscape to LaserGRBL to UGS toolpath workflow
  \item Validated across materials, achieving clean vector plotting and wood engraving, and characterizing edge-quality limits on paper, cardboard, and cloth
\end{itemize}
\repo{https://github.com/Rifat137710/Versatile-CNC-Machine-Pen-plotter-Cutter-Engraver-}

\entry{Stock Market Prediction with Sentiment Analysis}{January 2025}
<<Course Name>> (EEE 402), Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology \quad|\quad Team of <<N>>
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built an end-to-end forecasting pipeline fusing daily financial-news sentiment with price history and ten technical indicators (MACD, RSI-14, Bollinger Bands, EMA, log momentum) to predict GOOG closing prices over a six-year window

\entry{Battery Charge Controller using Buck Converter with Auto Cut-Off}{July 2024}
Power Electronics Laboratory (EEE 316), Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology \quad|\quad Team of 5
\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed and built a mains-to-battery charger with adjustable output, implementing the full power chain from 230~V AC through a step-down transformer, bridge rectifier, 100~mH/1000~$\mu$F LC filter, and IRF250N buck stage
  \item Verified buck regulation on an oscilloscope at 25\%, 50\%, and 90\% duty cycle, confirming $V_o = k \cdot V_{in}$ tracking across the operating range
  \item Implemented Arduino-based auto cut-off using five-sample averaged ADC sensing with dual hysteresis thresholds, eliminating relay chatter at the switching boundary
\end{itemize}
\repo{https://github.com/Rifat137710/Battery-Charge-Controller-using-Buck-Converter-with-Auto-Cut-off-Feature-}
