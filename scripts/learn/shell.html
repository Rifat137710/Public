What the trained agents actually did

Read the last two columns together. The bottom row posts the best safety score in the whole evaluation and is the only method that turns a profit — and it put zero kilowatt-hours into a battery all day. It is an agent trained on a stronger grid than the one it was tested on, and its entire throughput was discharge: it sold the drivers’ own batteries back to the grid. Nobody arranged that. It was measured.

Violation rate here counts steps in which any street was out of range, out of 288 five-minute steps in a day, and drivers charged is over the ~27 who departed during an episode. The simulator above counts street-steps out of 33 × 288 and scores all 287 cars, so the two are not meant to be read against each other.
